# 新版六轴图B

**中文：** 电机停转命令下力传感器过载补偿的离线计算结果。(a)—(c)分别采用名义1、5、10 s加速的未补偿原始记录，上排为补偿前输出T，下排为按所提供源码规则离线计算的输出 $\hat T$。两排使用相同二状态卡尔曼滤波流程及参数。原始sensor2按0.00980665 N/g换算，各记录以最初20个已记录样本估计共享零偏b0；补偿量由固定系数−36.839、3.332 g/(m/s²)与固定30 ms历史加速度计算。每列所有通道统一以自身来流的 $t_{10}$ 为零点，浅灰阴影表示实测10%—90%加速段，长度分别为0.797、4.038和8.096 s。六轴采用相同纵轴范围，黑色水平虚线为零参考。图内RMSE在 $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s内相对于零计算，单位N。

**必要注：** 源码对应论文式(23)的轴向定义为 $a_z=-a_y$、径向为正 $a_x$，有效系数约为0.361267与0.032676 kg；历史加速度按源码规则取不晚于目标时刻的最新已记录样本，未拟合时延。上下两排共用同一个b0，不分别去均值或采用不同滤波强度。结果是当前源码在可用日志采样上的离线应用，不是原始飞控记录的在线补偿输出或逐位固件复现。系数标定来源和独立性未确认；1 s仍存在约−0.91 N的窗口均值偏差。

**English:** Offline overload compensation of force-sensor outputs under a motor-stop command. Panels (a)–(c) use the uncompensated raw recordings for nominal acceleration times of 1, 5, and 10 s. The upper row shows the output T before compensation, and the lower row shows $\hat T$ reconstructed using the provided source-code rules. Both rows use the same two-state Kalman-filter procedure and parameters. Raw sensor2 values are converted at 0.00980665 N/g; each recording uses a shared bias b0 estimated from its first 20 logged samples. Compensation uses fixed coefficients of −36.839 and 3.332 g/(m/s²), together with the fixed 30 ms acceleration-history rule. All channels in a recording share its inflow-derived $t_{10}$ origin. Gray shading marks the measured 10%–90% acceleration intervals of 0.797, 4.038, and 8.096 s. All six axes use common vertical limits, and black horizontal dashed lines indicate zero. RMSE is evaluated relative to zero over $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s, in N.

**Required note:** The source corresponds to Eq. (23) with axial acceleration $a_z=-a_y$ and positive radial $a_x$, giving effective coefficients of approximately 0.361267 and 0.032676 kg. Historical acceleration is the latest logged sample no later than the source-defined target time; no delay is fitted. Before and after signals share the same b0 and filter strength, without independent recentering. These curves are offline applications of the current source to available logged samples, not recorded online outputs or bit-exact firmware replays. Calibration provenance and independence are unconfirmed. The 1 s trial retains an approximately −0.91 N window-mean bias.

# 单独1 s图

**中文：** 真实1 s加速记录的源码规则离线补偿对照。上排为未补偿输出，下排为离线补偿输出，两者采用同一零偏、原生力时间戳和二状态卡尔曼滤波流程。补偿表达式为 $\hat T=0.00980665[s-b_0+36.839a_y(t-0.03)-3.332a_x(t-0.03)]$，未对系数、符号或时延进行优化。灰色阴影为实测0.797 s的10%—90%加速段；评价窗口为 $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s。RMSE由2.45降至1.02 N，最大绝对输出由5.63降至2.36 N；补偿后仍有约−0.91 N均值偏差。该结果为离线计算，不是独立在线验证。

**English:** Source-based offline compensation of the actual 1 s recording. Upper and lower axes show uncompensated and offline compensated outputs, respectively, with identical bias handling, native force timestamps, and two-state Kalman filtering. Compensation is $\hat T=0.00980665[s-b_0+36.839a_y(t-0.03)-3.332a_x(t-0.03)]$, without optimization of coefficients, signs, or delay. Gray shading marks the measured 0.797 s 10%–90% acceleration interval. Over $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s, RMSE decreases from 2.45 to 1.02 N and maximum absolute output from 5.63 to 2.36 N; an approximately −0.91 N mean bias remains. This is an offline calculation, not independent online validation.
