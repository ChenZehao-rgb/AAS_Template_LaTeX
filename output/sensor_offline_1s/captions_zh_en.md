# 新版三列两行图B

**中文：** 停转命令下力传感器输出的补偿前后对照。(a)采用真实未补偿1 s记录，按式(23)及固定系数A=0.361 kg、B=0.033 kg离线计算补偿；(b)、(c)保留文件标称5、10 s记录的在线补偿滤波输出。上排为同记录原始 `pressure_force_sensor.sensor2` 按项目约定换算的未补偿输出，下排为补偿后输出。各列全部通道分别以本试验来流达到10%增量的时刻 $t_{10}$ 对齐；浅灰阴影为实测10%—90%加速段，三列长度分别为0.797、4.093和7.960 s。黑色水平虚线为零参考，竖线为 $\tau=0$。六轴使用相同纵轴尺度，每列上下使用相同时间范围。图内RMSE均相对于零，在 $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s内计算。

**必要注：** (a)轴向加速度取日志正号y轴，径向取正号x轴，各扣除自身扰动前 $[t_{10}-3,t_{10}-0.5]$ s均值；使用IMU `timestamp_sample` 并线性插值至原生力采样时刻，不调整时延、不改变系数和符号。1 s补偿前后均无额外滤波，也未分别去除力均值；5、10 s下排仍含在线滤波。因此三列不构成相同滤波链的独立验证。在当前确认的轴向正号下，1 s离线补偿增大了负向峰值，不能据此声称快速加速补偿有效。力换算沿用0.0098 N/读数的项目约定，未重新核实本记录的固件转换。

**English:** Force-sensor outputs before and after compensation under a motor-stop command. (a) uses the actual uncompensated 1 s recording and offline application of Eq. (23), with fixed coefficients A=0.361 kg and B=0.033 kg; (b) and (c) retain the logged online compensated and filtered outputs from the file-labeled 5 s and 10 s recordings. The upper row shows each recording's raw `pressure_force_sensor.sensor2` output converted using the project convention, and the lower row shows the compensated output. All channels in each recording share its own inflow-derived $t_{10}$ origin. Gray shading denotes the measured 10%–90% acceleration intervals of 0.797, 4.093, and 7.960 s. Horizontal black dashed lines indicate zero, and vertical lines indicate $\tau=0$. All six axes use common vertical limits; paired axes use identical time ranges. RMSE is relative to zero and evaluated over $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s.

**Required note:** For (a), axial and radial accelerations use the logged positive y and x axes, respectively, after subtraction of their pre-disturbance means over $[t_{10}-3,t_{10}-0.5]$ s. IMU `timestamp_sample` values are linearly interpolated to native force timestamps, without fitted delay, coefficient changes, or sign changes. Both 1 s signals receive no additional filtering or separate force recentering, whereas the lower 5 s and 10 s signals include online filtering. The columns therefore do not constitute filter-matched independent validation. Under the confirmed axis signs, offline compensation increases the negative 1 s force peak; this result does not demonstrate effective compensation during rapid acceleration. Force conversion retains the project's 0.0098 N per sensor-unit convention and is not reverified against this recording's firmware.

# 单独1 s对照图

**中文：** 真实1 s加速记录中式(23)的离线应用。上、下轴分别为原始换算输出T及 $\hat T=T-0.361a_z-0.033a_x$，二者均不额外滤波、保留相同的原生力时间戳。$a_z$ 和 $a_x$ 分别取用户确认的正号IMU y、x轴，并扣除扰动前均值后同步到力采样时刻。统一以自身来流 $t_{10}$ 为零点，浅灰阴影为实测0.797 s的10%—90%加速段，黑色虚线为零参考。评价窗口 $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s内，补偿前后RMSE分别为2.31和3.90 N，最大绝对输出分别为5.70和10.34 N。当前符号定义下补偿增大负峰，图示结果为离线公式检查，不是在线补偿效果验证。

**English:** Offline application of Eq. (23) to the actual 1 s acceleration recording. The upper and lower axes show the converted raw output T and $\hat T=T-0.361a_z-0.033a_x$, respectively, without additional filtering and with identical native force timestamps. Axial and radial accelerations use the user-confirmed positive IMU y and x axes after removal of their pre-disturbance means and synchronization to force samples. Both traces share the inflow-derived $t_{10}$ origin. Gray shading marks the measured 0.797 s 10%–90% acceleration interval, and black dashed lines mark zero. Over $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s, RMSE changes from 2.31 to 3.90 N and maximum absolute output from 5.70 to 10.34 N. With the current sign convention, compensation increases the negative peak. This is an offline formula check, not verification of recorded online compensation effectiveness.
