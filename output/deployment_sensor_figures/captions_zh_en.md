## 新图A：转速—控制量静态映射及动态来流下的转速变化

**中文：** 转速—控制量静态映射与固定控制量下的动态转速变化。(a) 两次升降扫描中的四组静态标定结果及各自的二次拟合曲线，拟合模型为 $u_{\mathrm{cal}}=a\Omega^2+b\Omega+c$，其中 $\Omega=2\pi n/60$，单位为 rad/s。各组使用导出的平台均值，相邻近重复控制点按 $|\Delta u|\leq0.002$ 归并；四组实际控制量点位不完全相同，因此在共同区间 $u_{\mathrm{cal}}\in[0.18777,0.89996]$ 内分段线性插值比较转速，最大组间相对极差为4.20%。(b) 名义1 s来流加速试验中，三个固定执行指令下的转速相对变化 $\delta n=(n-n_{\mathrm{pre}})/n_{\mathrm{pre}}\times100\%$。图例为日志 `thrust_control.control[0]` 中的固定归一化执行指令 $u_{0,\mathrm{cmd}}$，相应最终PWM分别为1486、1582和1663 μs。各次试验分别以自身来流的 $t_{10}$ 对齐，$n_{\mathrm{pre}}$ 为自身 $[t_{10}-3,t_{10}-0.5]$ s内的平均机械转速；转速保留原始采样波动。右轴细灰虚线和浅灰阴影均取自中间控制量 $u_{0,\mathrm{cmd}}=0.530$ 的实测来流，阴影为该试验的10%—90%加速段（0—0.8198 s），不代表完整统计窗口。三次试验的10%—90%加速时间为0.8060—0.8198 s，对齐来流的最大两两差约0.265 m/s。黑色零线与±5%水平虚线用于比较，±5%不是置信区间。

**必须保留的注：** CSV中的 $u_{\mathrm{cal}}$ 处理节点无法由现有两列数据确认，故与动态试验的 $u_{\mathrm{cmd}}$ 分开标记，不假设两者可直接互换。四组为两次循环的上、下沿，不是四次独立重复试验。CSV缺少平台内时间序列，不能重新核查过渡段剔除或绘制采样误差棒。高来流稳态统计窗口为 $[t_{90}+2,t_{90}+3]$ s，扰动统计窗口为 $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s。1 s三个工况的稳态均值偏差均小于5%，但瞬态最大绝对偏差均超过5%。

**English:** Static rotor-speed–command mapping and rotor-speed changes under fixed commands. (a) Four ascending/descending sweeps from two calibration cycles, with separate quadratic fits $u_{\mathrm{cal}}=a\Omega^2+b\Omega+c$, where $\Omega=2\pi n/60$ in rad/s. Exported plateau means at adjacent near-duplicate commands ($|\Delta u|\leq0.002$) are combined. Because the recorded command levels differ, speeds are compared by piecewise-linear interpolation over the shared interval $u_{\mathrm{cal}}\in[0.18777,0.89996]$, yielding a maximum relative inter-sweep range of 4.20%. (b) Relative mechanical rotor-speed change, $\delta n=(n-n_{\mathrm{pre}})/n_{\mathrm{pre}}\times100\%$, during nominal 1 s inflow acceleration at three fixed applied normalized commands logged in `thrust_control.control[0]`; the corresponding final PWM outputs are 1486, 1582, and 1663 μs. Each trial is aligned at its own inflow $t_{10}$, and $n_{\mathrm{pre}}$ is its mean speed over $[t_{10}-3,t_{10}-0.5]$ s. Native speed fluctuations are retained. The thin gray dashed inflow trace and shaded 10%–90% acceleration interval (0–0.8198 s) use the middle-command trial, $u_{0,\mathrm{cmd}}=0.530$. The three measured acceleration intervals span 0.8060–0.8198 s, and the largest pairwise difference between aligned inflow traces is approximately 0.265 m/s. The zero line and horizontal ±5% dashed lines are comparison references, not confidence limits.

**Required note:** The processing node represented by the static CSV's $u_{\mathrm{cal}}$ is unconfirmed; it is distinguished from $u_{\mathrm{cmd}}$ rather than assumed interchangeable. The four sweeps are not four independent repetitions. Native plateau samples are unavailable, preventing a renewed transition-exclusion audit or sampling error bars. Steady-state and disturbance statistics use $[t_{90}+2,t_{90}+3]$ s and $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s, respectively. For these three trials, steady-state mean deviations are below 5%, whereas transient maximum absolute deviations exceed 5%.

## 新图B：电机停转命令下传感器原始输出与在线补偿滤波输出

**中文：** 电机停转命令下的力传感器输出对照。三列分别对应文件标称(a)1 s、(b)5 s和(c)10 s加速条件；每列上下信号取自同一次已补偿记录。上排为 `pressure_force_sensor.sensor2` 按项目既有克力换算约定 $T=0.0098\,\mathrm{sensor2}$ 得到的未补偿原始输出，下排为 `thrust_data.thrust_kalman_filter_data_1` 记录的在线补偿及滤波输出。各列全部通道统一以自身来流的 $t_{10}$ 为时间零点，不按力峰值重新对齐。来流按 $V_\infty=1.5|\omega_z|$ 计算，仅对来流使用与现有图8—12相同的0.35 s中值检测流程。浅灰阴影表示实测10%—90%加速段，竖线为 $\tau=0$，黑色水平虚线为零参考。六轴采用相同纵轴范围及刻度，同列上下使用相同时间范围 $[-3,t_{90}-t_{10}+4]$ s。图内RMSE均相对于零参考、在 $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s内计算，单位为N。

**必须保留的注：** (a)的星号表示文件名异常：三列实测10%—90%加速时间分别为4.083、4.093和7.960 s，因此(a)不能解释为真实1 s快速加速的补偿验证。未补偿1 s记录的实测加速段为0.797 s，但没有与之匹配的已补偿快速记录。原始通道的克力单位沿用项目表格约定，本批ULog的比例、符号和固件零偏未重新核实；图中不另行去零或添加离线滤波。下排含在线滤波，而上排保留原始采样，因此该图展示“原始换算输出—在线补偿滤波输出”，其改善幅度不能单独归因于式(23)补偿。系数来源和试验独立性未确认，不称为独立验证。PWM均为停转指令1000 μs，但日志中仍有少量非零编码器读数，未以数据删减方式强制置零。

**English:** Force-sensor outputs under a motor-stop command. Columns correspond to file-labeled acceleration conditions of (a) 1 s, (b) 5 s, and (c) 10 s; the upper and lower signals in each column come from the same compensated recording. The upper row shows the uncorrected raw output from `pressure_force_sensor.sensor2`, converted using the project's gram-force convention, $T=0.0098\,\mathrm{sensor2}$; the lower row shows the logged online compensated and filtered output, `thrust_data.thrust_kalman_filter_data_1`. All channels in a trial share its inflow-derived $t_{10}$ origin, without force-peak realignment. Inflow is calculated as $V_\infty=1.5|\omega_z|$; only the inflow detector uses the existing 0.35 s median-filter procedure. Gray shading marks each measured 10%–90% acceleration interval, the vertical line marks $\tau=0$, and the horizontal black dashed line marks zero. All six axes use identical vertical limits and ticks; paired axes use the same interval, $[-3,t_{90}-t_{10}+4]$ s. RMSE annotations are relative to zero and evaluated over $[t_{10},t_{90}+3]$ s, in N.

**Required note:** The asterisk in (a) flags a filename inconsistency: measured 10%–90% intervals are 4.083, 4.093, and 7.960 s. Panel (a) therefore does not validate compensation during a true 1 s acceleration. The separate uncompensated 1 s recording has a 0.797 s interval, with no matched compensated fast recording available. The raw-channel unit conversion follows the project workbook convention; its applicability, firmware sign and offset are not reverified for these ULogs. No offline recentering or additional filtering is applied. Because the lower row includes online filtering while the upper row retains raw samples, the improvement represents the combined recorded processing chain and cannot be attributed solely to Eq. (23). Coefficient provenance and trial independence are unconfirmed; this is not presented as independent validation. All selected recordings have a 1000 μs motor-stop PWM, but sparse nonzero encoder readings remain and are not forcibly removed.
